REAL MADRID · STORY

CLUB HISTORY

A few important moments in the club's history.

THE BEGINNING

Founded in 1902

Madrid Foot Ball Club was founded on 6 March 1902. In 1920, the club received the title “Real”, which means “Royal” in Spanish.

Today Real Madrid has supporters in many countries.

IMPORTANT YEARS

Short timeline

  1. 1902

    The beginning

    Madrid Foot Ball Club is founded.

  2. 1920

    The royal name

    The club receives the name “Real Madrid”.

  3. 1947

    A new stadium

    The Nuevo Chamartín stadium opens.

  4. 1956

    European Cup

    Real Madrid wins the first European Cup.

THEN AND NOW

The stadium changed

The stadium has been updated over the years. It is still an important part of the club.
