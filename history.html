WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

MADRID.
6 MARCH 1902.

A white shirt. A new beginning.

Madrid Foot Ball Club was founded on 6 March 1902. In 1920, the club received the title “Real”, meaning “Royal” in Spanish.

Generations of players and supporters have carried that story forward.

THE YEARS THAT SHAPED US

A STORY WORTH TELLING

  1. 1902

    The beginning

    Madrid Foot Ball Club is founded on 6 March.

  2. 1920

    A royal name

    The title “Real” becomes part of the club’s identity.

  3. 1947

    A new home

    The Nuevo Chamartín stadium opens. It is renamed Santiago Bernabéu in 1955.

  4. 1956

    The first European Cup

    Real Madrid wins the first edition of the competition.

  5. 2024

    La Decimoquinta

    A 2–0 win over Borussia Dortmund at Wembley brings a fifteenth European Cup.
