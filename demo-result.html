How the practice forms work

The Stadium enquiry checks your entries and shows a confirmation; it does not send a message or book tickets. Fan Club Sign Up saves a practice account in this browser. Log In checks its email and password, then opens your Profile.

Use sample details and a disposable password. Practice accounts stay on this device until you clear the site data. Log out ends the current demo session. This is a classroom simulation, not secure authentication or official club membership.
